# Battleboard v0.4 — Chapter One Demo

## Demo promise

A new player can begin from a title screen, understand why they are building a board, recruit and develop the missing fighters, assemble a legal eight-position Bronze Board, enter the Ashline qualifier, resolve contested squares through direct control, and finish Chapter One with persistent consequences.

## Chapter One: No Board, No Entry

The player arrives at Grand Tournament registration without a board and is refused entry. Goro reframes the problem: Bronze circuit requires eight positions, not a full sixteen. The starting stable supplies five. The remaining Queen, Rook and Pawn are player decisions.

## Guided flow

1. Opening registration story.
2. Scout and recruit until stable reaches eight.
3. Train at least one fighter for the role they will hold.
4. Assign King, Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight and three Pawns.
5. Enter the Ashline Local Qualifier.
6. Win and receive the Bronze registration seal.

The guide explains the current decision but does not pick recruits or roles for the player.

## Persistence

Save schema v4 preserves roster, assignments, training sessions, progression, injuries, relationships, record, and Chapter One story state. v0.3 saves remain loadable and are migrated into the demo flow.
