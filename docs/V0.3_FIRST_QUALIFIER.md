# Battleboard v0.3 — First Qualification Match

v0.3 turns the visual proof into the first campaign loop.

1. Begin with five inherited fighters and an incomplete Bronze Board.
2. Scout a larger candidate pool.
3. Spend crowns to recruit fighters.
4. Select any stable member, choose one of six board roles, and train that role.
5. Assign exactly eight active fighters: King, Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight, three Pawns.
6. Enter the Local Qualifier against the Ashline Gate Board.
7. Alternate tactical turns with deterministic opponent planning.
8. Every contested square becomes the existing direct-control encounter.
9. The player's nearby affinity network can visibly intervene.
10. Defeat the opposing King to win the qualifier.
11. Return to HQ with crowns, reputation, training tokens, XP, relationships and persistent injury history.
12. Campaign state saves locally to `user://battleboard_v03_save.json`.

The reduced Bronze Board is the onboarding licence. The full 16-fighter board remains the later competitive target.
